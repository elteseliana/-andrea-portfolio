FAQ

Good questions.

How long does a detail take?

A Wash takes about two hours. Polish is usually a full day, and Ceramic needs two to three days so the coating can cure properly.

Do you pick up and drop off?

Yes, within our service area. Add your address in the booking notes and we'll confirm a pick-up window.

How long does a ceramic coating last?

With a proper maintenance wash every few weeks, years rather than months. We'll walk you through aftercare at pick-up.

Will polishing remove all scratches?

Swirls and light scratches, yes. Anything that has gone through the clear coat needs paint work; we'll tell you honestly at inspection.

How do I pay?

Card or cash at pick-up. No deposit is needed for Wash or Polish.
